# M6 — legacy routes the customer app calls that the contract does not declare

Generated by `tool/integration_probe` from `servana_api_client.dart` call sites,
diffed against the v1 contract's own `legacy` mappings. Regenerate, do not edit.

Comparison is on route **shape**, not literal text: the contract writes
`:bookingId` and a call site yields a concrete id, so both normalise to `:id`
first. Comparing the literal text counted those as different routes and
inflated this list from 30 to 50.

**30 of 70** routes the shipped client constructs have no entry in
`V1_CONTRACT[].legacy`. They have no disposition, no canonical successor and no
place on the deprecation clock — the migration matrix cannot see them and
`deprecation.ts` cannot signpost them.

**This is not a client defect.** Each needs a contract disposition: `KEEP`,
`ALIAS_TEMPORARILY`, `CANONICALIZE`, `ROLE_SPECIFIC` or `RETIRE`.

It matters because 94 routes are already `ALIAS_TEMPORARILY` with
`Deprecation: true` live in production. When those retire, these are the ones
nobody counted.

`status` is an unauthenticated probe against production, 2026-08-18. `401` means
mounted and gated; `429` is the auth limiter. Neither means broken.
